01 / THE BIG IDEA

One feature.
Three responsibilities.

Location data can reveal where someone lives, studies, works, worships, or spends free time. A responsible app developer should think about more than whether a feature works: they should consider consent, safety, transparency, and the rules that apply.

The goal is not to ban useful technology. It is to use personal information in a way people can understand and reasonably control.

THREE LENSES

Safety, ethics, and law are connected—but different.

◈

01 · SAFETY

Reduce possible harm

Unexpected location sharing can expose a person's routines or make it easier for strangers to find them.

Ask: Could this feature put someone at risk?
◎

02 · ETHICS

Choose what is fair

Ethical design respects privacy, explains data use clearly, asks for meaningful permission, and avoids collecting data that is not needed.

Ask: Is this respectful and fair?
§

03 · LAW

Follow legal rules

In the United States, Section 5 of the Federal Trade Commission Act prohibits unfair or deceptive acts or practices in or affecting commerce.

Ask: Could this break a rule or mislead users?

Important distinction: Something can be a poor ethical choice without automatically being illegal. Whether conduct violates a law depends on the facts and applicable rules.
